Our Services – Camps & Distribution

Direct help reaching villages and schools – medicamp, health camps and stationery distribution.

Free Medicamp

General health and non-communicable disease (NCD) check-up camps.

  • Blood pressure, pulse rate and oxygen check
  • Random blood sugar (RBS) test
  • Doctor consultation and free generic medicines

Health Camp (Eye Care)

Eye check-up, spectacles and support for cataract surgery.

  • Vision testing by a certified optometrist
  • Cataract and glaucoma screening
  • Free reading glasses for elders; hospital referral when needed

Stationery in Schools

Study material given to needy students right at their school.

  • Notebooks, pens, pencils and other stationery
  • School bags and study material
  • A pledge that no child misses out on learning
New Project · DPR

Drishti & Arogya Seva Abhiyan

Integrated health and eye care in rural Bihar

Screening, cataract surgery and free spectacles through 24 mega camps in Siwan district and nearby blocks – a 12-month pilot.

Download DPR (PDF)
Total budget₹29,85,000
Duration12 months (pilot)
AreaSiwan district & nearby blocks
BeneficiariesAged 50+, BPL families, elders, women
24Mega camps

66.2%of blindness among people above 50 is caused by untreated cataract (National Blindness Survey 2015-19, as per the DPR).

Why this project matters

  • Economic inability (22.1%)Families below the poverty line cannot afford surgery and lenses.
  • Lack of awareness (18.4%)Weak vision is often accepted as a normal part of ageing.
  • Geographic isolationElders cannot travel to city centres without family.

Four Project Components

A

General Health & NCD Screening

BP, pulse, oxygen and blood sugar tests; 5-day generic medicines free.

B

Comprehensive Eye Check-up

Snellen chart, auto-refractometer, trial lens and slit-lamp evaluation.

C

Cataract Surgery & Referral

Free SICS+IOL at accredited hospitals; free transport, food, medicines, dark glasses; follow-ups on day 1, 7, 30.

D

Free Spectacles

Reading glasses on the spot for elders; custom glasses in 10 days at the Panchayat Bhawan.

Where the budget goes

ANon-recurring equipment (diagnostic/screening kits)₹3,00,000
BCamp operations, village mobilisation, medicines (24 camps)₹5,04,000
CDirect clinical work (800 surgeries + 3,600 spectacles)₹18,90,000
DCoordination, HR, MIS and administration₹2,91,000
Total project budget₹29,85,000

Quality assurance: surgeries are performed only in operation theatres of accredited hospitals under strict infection control – never in the camps.

Project Sanjeevani

A transparent health-support initiative for poor families fighting serious illness

Our Special Initiative

No life should be lost for lack of treatment

Many poor families in Siwan and nearby areas cannot afford costly treatment such as chemotherapy. Project Sanjeevani aims to give such patients help with treatment, nutrition and proper guidance.

  • Support only after verifying medical documents, with full transparency
  • Help meant for treatment – routed directly to the hospital wherever possible
  • Needy patients being treated at reputed hospitals such as AIIMS and Tata Memorial
  • Monthly nutrition support (fruits, protein, nuts) and proper counselling
Support the ProjectLearn More

How It Works

1. Identify the need

Reaching patients from poor families in Siwan and nearby areas who are fighting serious illness.

2. Verify documents

Checking medical documents so that every case is handled transparently.

3. Direct support

Help is meant for treatment, routed towards the hospital wherever possible.

4. Nutrition & counselling

Aiming to provide nutrition support and proper counselling to patients.
